Stage 7 of 10

Clear to close — the 2-hour review window

The 2-hour window after CTC. Settlement statement review, comp verification, cash to close confirmation, insurance binding, title coordination.

What this stage is: Settlement statement review, 2-hour rule.

PHASE 7: CLEAR TO CLOSE

When CTC drops:

Immediate actions (within 2 hours):

  1. Review the settlement statement / closing disclosure
  • Verify loan amount, rate, term match what was quoted
  • Verify YOUR comp is correct:
  • Borrower-paid origination points: $[X]
  • Lender-paid YSP: $[X] (may show as "broker compensation" or "lender credit")
  • Processing fee: $[X]
  • Admin fee: $[X]
  • Verify borrower's cash to close is correct
  • Check for any surprise lender fees that weren't disclosed
  1. Coordinate with title company
  • Confirm closing date, time, location
  • Confirm title company has the closing package from the lender
  • Confirm title insurance premium and endorsements
  • Insurance referral: If you have a referral arrangement, confirm with the insurance agent that the policy is bound and your referral is documented
  • Get wire instructions FROM the title company (never create your own)
  1. Notify the borrower

    2. > "Hey [Name], great news — your loan is clear to close! Here's what happens next: > > Closing date: [date] > Location: [title company name + address] > Time: [time] > > What to bring: > - Government-issued photo ID > - Cashier's check for $[cash to close] payable to [title company name] > (OR wire instructions — I'll send those separately from the title company) > - Entity documents (if closing in LLC): [specific docs] > > Wire fraud warning: NEVER trust wire instructions received via email alone. > Always call the title company directly to verify before wiring funds. > Use a phone number you looked up yourself, not one from an email. > > Questions? Call me: [phone]"

  1. Wire instructions handling
  • Get wire instructions from the title company directly
  • Forward to borrower via email
  • CALL the borrower and confirm they received it
  • Tell them to CALL the title company to verify before wiring
  • Wire fraud is the #1 risk in real estate closings — protect your client and yourself
