Stage 8 of 10

Closing — the signing order, the funding, the recording

What happens at the table. The signing order, the funding sequence, the recording, and when the broker gets paid. What to bring. What NOT to do the week before.

What this stage is: Signing order, funding, recording.

PHASE 8: CLOSING

Day of closing:

Pre-closing (morning of):

  • [ ] Confirm with title company: "Are we still on for [time] today?"
  • [ ] Confirm with borrower: "See you at [time] — have everything you need?"
  • [ ] If remote close (mobile notary): confirm notary is scheduled

At closing:

  • [ ] You do NOT need to attend every closing (most brokers don't)
  • [ ] If you attend: review docs with borrower, answer questions, be helpful
  • [ ] If you don't attend: be available by phone for any questions during signing
  • [ ] Signing takes 30-60 minutes typically
  • [ ] Borrower signs: promissory note, deed of trust/mortgage, closing disclosure (if provided), entity resolutions (if LLC), hazard insurance acknowledgment, title documents

Post-signing same day:

  • [ ] Title company sends signed package to lender for review
  • [ ] Lender reviews and authorizes funding (same day or next day)
  • [ ] Title company disburses funds:
  • Seller (purchase) or payoff (refinance)
  • Lender origination fees
  • Title/escrow fees
  • Insurance premiums
  • YOUR COMP (broker fees, processing, YSP)
  • Property taxes (if escrowed)

If funding is delayed:

  • Most business purpose loans fund same day or next day
  • If delayed: call the lender and the title company
  • Common delay reasons: lender QC review, missing satisfaction for payoff, title issues discovered at signing
  • Push hard — every day of delay costs the borrower (per diem interest)
