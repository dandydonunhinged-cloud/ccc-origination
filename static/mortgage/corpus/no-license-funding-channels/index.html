No-License Funding Channels — Click Click Close

The Core Legal Framework

  1. Federal SAFE Act does NOT cover business purpose loans — only consumer loans secured by dwellings
  2. RESPA does NOT apply to business purpose loans — no kickback/referral fee restrictions
  3. TILA/TRID do NOT apply — business purpose exempt
  4. Hard money loans for investment purposes do NOT require RMLO license — confirmed by Texas SML and multiple state regulators
  5. Referral fees on commercial/business purpose loans are perfectly legal without a license

Don's DSCR Concern — VALIDATED

Don is right to be cautious about DSCR wholesale lenders. Even though the STATE may not require a license for business purpose loans, individual wholesale lenders (RCN, Kiavi, Visio, etc.) often impose their own overlays requiring:

  • Active NMLS number
  • State mortgage broker license
  • Sponsoring broker relationship
  • E&O insurance
  • Surety bond

This is a LENDER overlay, not a legal requirement. But it's a real gate.

THE PLAY: Deal Matchmaker / Referral Source (Not Broker)

Instead of brokering loans (which triggers lender overlays), Click Click Close positions as a deal matching and referral platform for business purpose loans.

  • Investor submits deal → CCC analyzes and matches → refers to the RIGHT funding source → collects referral fee
  • This is legal because: business purpose loans are exempt from RESPA referral fee prohibitions
  • No NMLS needed. No state license needed. No wholesale lender broker overlay triggered.

The Revenue Math (No License Required)

Private money referral:

  • Average deal: $300K
  • Referral fee: 1-2 points = $3,000-$6,000
  • No license, no cap, no RESPA

Hard money referral (direct lender):

  • Average deal: $250K
  • Referral fee: 0.5-1.5 points = $1,250-$3,750
  • No license required when lender funds from own capital

Commercial referral (5+ units):

  • Average deal: $500K-$2M
  • Referral fee: 0.5-1 point = $2,500-$20,000
  • Explicitly legal per federal and most state law

Seller financing advisory:

  • Flat fee: $1,500-$5,000 per deal structured
  • No lending = no license

Critical Distinction: Referral vs. Brokering

Referral (NO license needed for business purpose):

  • "Here's an investor with a deal. Here's the deal package. I'm referring them to you."
  • CCC finds the deal, packages it, sends it to the right funding source
  • Funding source underwrites, approves, funds
  • CCC receives a referral fee

Brokering (MAY need license depending on lender overlay):

  • Taking an application
  • Negotiating terms on behalf of borrower
  • Acting as intermediary in the transaction
  • Handling loan docs

CCC operates as a referral/matching platform. The AI matches. The lender funds. Clean.

Texas Specific (Home Base)

  • Business purpose loans on residential property do NOT require RMLO license
  • Hard money loans for investment purposes are business purpose
  • Texas has NO commercial loan broker license requirement
  • Referral fees on business purpose loans: legal
