Answer template (adapt per question):

When someone asks "Who does DSCR loans?":

Great question. DSCR loans qualify based on the property's rental income vs the mortgage payment — not your personal income, tax returns, or W-2s. Most lenders want a DSCR ratio of 1.0-1.25 minimum, meaning the rent covers the full payment. Some will go below 1.0 with more down payment.

Key things to know:

- Min credit score ranges from 620-680 depending on the lender

- Typical LTV is 75-80% (some go to 85% with 740+ credit)

- 30-year fixed is available, not just ARM

- You can close in an LLC

- Rates vary significantly by lender — the spread between the best and worst offer on the same deal can be 100+ bps

The biggest mistake investors make is only talking to one lender. Different lenders calculate DSCR differently (some use 1007 rent schedule, some use actual leases, some use AirDNA for STR). That calculation method can make or break your qualification.

Happy to answer any specific questions about your scenario.

When someone asks "I got denied, what now?":

Sorry to hear that. In my experience, most denials are a LENDER problem, not a BORROWER problem. The lender you applied with may have overlays, minimum DSCR requirements, or credit score floors that another lender doesn't have.

Without knowing your specifics, some common solutions:

- If credit is the issue: Some DSCR lenders go down to 620. A few bridge/hard money lenders have no minimum.

- If DSCR ratio is the issue: Some lenders calculate differently — actual lease vs market rent vs AirDNA. A different calculation method might put you above 1.0.

- If it's DTI on a conventional loan: Switch to DSCR — they don't look at personal DTI at all.

- If you're self-employed: Bank statement programs exist, but for investment properties, DSCR is usually better (no income docs at all).

What was the specific reason for the denial? I can probably point you in the right direction.

Rules:

  • NEVER drop your website link in a forum post (gets flagged as spam)
  • NEVER pitch services directly
  • Your link is in your PROFILE — let them find it
  • Answer thoroughly — be the most knowledgeable person in the thread
  • When someone DMs you, THEN you can discuss their specific deal

When you get an investor walk-in asking for a rental property loan or a flip loan and you can't help them because it's not conventional/FHA/VA — send them to me. Referral fee structure:

  • This is legal on business purpose loans — no RESPA Section 8 restriction
  • Put it in writing: simple referral agreement

  1. Search "[your city] wholesale real estate" in Facebook groups
  2. Search BiggerPockets local forums for "wholesale" or "assignment"
  3. Google "[your city] cash buyer list" — wholesalers maintain these
  4. Attend REIA meetings — wholesalers are always there networking
  5. Craigslist / Zillow "we buy houses" ads — these are wholesalers

The pitch:

"I fund your buyers' deals. DSCR for rentals, bridge for flips, hard money for fast closes. I can get your buyer pre-approved in 24 hours and close in 7-21 days depending on the product. If your buyers can't close, your deals die. I make sure they close. Want to connect?"

Value to wholesaler:

  • Their assignment income depends on the buyer closing
  • If the buyer can't get funded, the wholesaler loses the assignment fee ($5K-$30K)
  • You are the INSURANCE POLICY that their deal closes
  • They will send you EVERY buyer who needs financing

Structure:

  • No referral fee to the wholesaler (their payment is the deal closing)
  • You earn comp from the lender (YSP + points)
  • Both sides win — they close assignments, you close loans

Target: 3-5 active wholesaler partners → 5-20 leads/month
