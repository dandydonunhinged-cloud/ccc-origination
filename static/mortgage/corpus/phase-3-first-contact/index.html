PHASE 3: FIRST CONTACT — Full Operational Build-Out

The 15-minute window that wins or loses the deal

RULE: CALL WITHIN 15 MINUTES

78% of borrowers go with the first person who responds. This is not a guideline. This is the business.

If you're awake and the phone is on, you call within 15 minutes. Period.

CALL SCRIPT — WEBSITE LEAD (form submission)

Ring ring.

"Hey [Name], this is Don Brown with Click Click Close. You just submitted a deal on our site — [loan type] on a [property type] in [state]. I've already run it through our system and I have some good news. Do you have about 5 minutes?"

If yes:

"So based on what you told us — $[value] property, $[loan amount] loan, [credit score] credit — you qualify for [number] different programs through our lending partners. The best option looks like a [loan type] with a rate in the [X.XX% - X.XX%] range, [term] term, closing in about [timeline]."

"Now I want to make sure the numbers are right. A few quick questions:"

1. "Is this a purchase or refinance?"

2. "Is the property already rented or will you be placing tenants?"

3. "What's the monthly rent — or expected rent if it's vacant?"

4. "Are you buying in your personal name or an LLC?"

5. "Have you been denied anywhere else on this deal?"

After qualifying:

"Great. Here's what I'd recommend as next steps. I'm going to email you a document checklist — it's short, especially for [loan type]. Once I have those docs, I can submit to our lending partners and have you a formal offer within [3-7] business days. Most of our [loan type] loans close in [timeline]. Sound good?"

"What's the best email for the checklist? And is this number the best way to reach you?"

Close:

"Perfect. You'll have that email in the next 30 minutes. If any questions come up before then, text me at this number — I respond fast. Talk soon."

CALL SCRIPT — REFERRAL LEAD (from LO, agent, wholesaler, etc.)

"Hey [Name], this is Don Brown. [Referrer name] at [company] sent me your info — said you're looking for [loan type] on an investment property. I specialize in exactly that. Do you have a few minutes to walk me through the deal?"

Then same qualification questions as above.

Important: Always credit the referrer. The borrower knowing you were referred builds trust AND reinforces the referral partner relationship.

CALL SCRIPT — BIGGERPOCKETS / FACEBOOK DM LEAD

"Hey [Name], this is Don — we were chatting on [BiggerPockets / Facebook]. You mentioned you're looking at [deal type]. I wanted to follow up and see if I can help. Can I ask a few questions about the deal?"

These leads are warmer — they've already seen your expertise in the forum. Be conversational, not salesy.

Text immediately:

"Hey [Name], Don Brown here from Click Click Close. I just analyzed your deal submission — looks like you have some strong options. Give me a call when you get a chance or let me know a good time to connect. [your phone number]"

WHAT TO NEVER SAY ON THE FIRST CALL

  1. Never name a specific lender. Say "lending partner" or "program."
  • Why: If they know the lender, they might go direct. You are the value — the matching, the knowledge, the access to 32 options.
  1. Never quote an exact rate. Say "range" — e.g., "7.25% to 7.75% range."
  • Why: Rates change daily. If you quote 7.25% and it's 7.50% when you lock, you've broken trust.
  1. Never promise approval. Say "based on what you've told me, you qualify for X programs."
  • Why: Appraisal could kill the deal. Credit could come back different. You haven't seen docs yet.
  1. Never badmouth another lender. If they got denied elsewhere, say "different lenders have different guidelines — what didn't work there might work perfectly here."
  1. Never collect SSN on the first call. Say "we don't pull credit until you choose a lender and authorize it."
  • Why: Builds trust. Differentiates you from every other broker who pulls credit on day 1.

WHAT TO ALWAYS SAY

  1. "I work for you, not the lender." This is the broker value proposition.
  2. "I have access to 32+ lending partners." Shows breadth.
  3. "We don't pull credit until you're ready." Removes fear.
  4. "No income docs on most investor programs." Removes friction.
  5. "I respond fast." Set the expectation, then deliver.

TRACKING THE CALL

After every first contact, update your Leads spreadsheet:

  • Status: Contacted
  • Call date/time
  • Outcome: interested / needs time / dead
  • Next action: send docs checklist / follow up [date] / close lead
  • Notes: any specifics about the deal, concerns, timeline
