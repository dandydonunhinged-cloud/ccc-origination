PHASE 4: DOCUMENT COLLECTION — Full Operational Build-Out

Get docs fast, get docs right, don't ask twice

RULE: SEND THE CHECKLIST WITHIN 30 MINUTES OF FIRST CONTACT

The borrower is hottest right after the call. Send the checklist while they're still thinking about you. Every hour you wait, conversion drops.

EMAIL TEMPLATE — DOCUMENT REQUEST

Subject: Your [Loan Type] loan — document checklist (quick & easy)

Hi [Name],

Great talking with you. As promised, here's the document checklist for your [loan type] loan on the [property type] in [state/city].

Good news: this is a short list. Business purpose loans don't require tax returns, W-2s, or income verification. Here's what we do need:

DOCUMENTS NEEDED:

[PASTE LOAN-TYPE-SPECIFIC LIST BELOW]

HOW TO SEND:

- Reply to this email with attachments (PDF, JPG, or PNG)

- Or text photos to me at [phone number]

- Or upload to: [secure upload link if you build one]

WHAT HAPPENS NEXT:

Once I have these items, I submit to our lending partners. You'll have a formal offer (term sheet) within 3-5 business days. Then you choose the best option and we close.

TIMELINE:

- Docs received → Submitted to lender: 1-2 days

- Lender review + appraisal: 7-14 days

- Clear to close: 14-21 days (DSCR) / 7-14 days (bridge/flip)

- Closing: [estimated date]

Questions? Call or text me anytime: [phone]

— Don Brown

DOCUMENT CHECKLISTS BY LOAN TYPE

DSCR (Rental Property — Purchase)

DSCR LOAN — DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Required:
□ Executed purchase contract (all pages, all signatures)
□ Current lease agreement OR market rent estimate (AirDNA/Rentometer printout)
□ 2 months bank statements (all pages, all accounts used for reserves/down payment)
□ Photo ID (driver's license or passport — front and back)
□ Entity documents (if purchasing in LLC):
  □ Articles of Organization
  □ Operating Agreement
  □ EIN Letter from IRS
□ Homeowner's insurance quote or binder (on the subject property)
□ Credit authorization form (we'll send this — DO NOT provide your SSN via email)

If applicable:
□ Proof of reserves (beyond down payment — typically 3-6 months PITIA)
□ Rent roll (if multi-unit with existing tenants)
□ Property management agreement (if third-party managed)
□ Condo HOA docs (budget, insurance, rules — if condo/townhome)

NOT NEEDED:
✗ Tax returns
✗ W-2s
✗ Pay stubs
✗ Employment verification
✗ Personal financial statement (on most DSCR programs)

DSCR (Rental Property — Refinance)

DSCR LOAN — REFINANCE CHECKLIST

Required:
□ Current mortgage statement (most recent)
□ Current lease agreement OR rent schedule
□ 2 months bank statements (all pages)
□ Photo ID
□ Entity documents (if property is in LLC)
□ Homeowner's insurance declaration page (current policy)
□ Property tax bill (current year)
□ Credit authorization form

If applicable:
□ Payoff statement from current lender (we can order this)
□ Rehab scope + receipts (if cash-out refi after renovation — for ARV appraisal)
□ Rent roll (multi-unit)
□ HOA docs (condo/townhome)

FIX & FLIP / BRIDGE (Purchase + Rehab)

FIX & FLIP — DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Required:
□ Executed purchase contract
□ Scope of work (SOW) — line-item rehab budget with costs
  (Template: room by room, item by item, labor + materials)
□ Contractor bids or estimates (if using a GC)
□ ARV comps — 3-5 comparable SOLD properties supporting your ARV estimate
□ 2 months bank statements (reserves + rehab funds)
□ Photo ID
□ Entity documents (if purchasing in LLC)
□ Builder's risk / renovation insurance quote
□ Credit authorization form

If experienced flipper:
□ Track record — prior project HUDs, settlement statements, or before/after photos
  (Lenders give better terms to experienced flippers — 3+ projects preferred)
□ Contractor resume / license (if required by state)

If ground-up or major gut rehab:
□ Plans and specifications
□ Permits (or permit application timeline)
□ Survey / site plan
□ Detailed draw schedule (what gets paid when)

NOT NEEDED:
✗ Tax returns
✗ W-2s
✗ Income verification (most bridge/flip lenders)

CONSTRUCTION (Ground-Up)

CONSTRUCTION LOAN — DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Required:
□ Plans and specifications (architectural drawings)
□ Construction budget — detailed line-item (hard costs + soft costs)
□ Draw schedule — when each phase gets funded
□ Building permits (or timeline for obtaining permits)
□ Contractor license (if required by state/municipality)
□ Contractor resume — previous projects
□ Lot ownership proof (deed) — or lot purchase contract if acquiring
□ Lot mortgage payoff statement (if encumbered)
□ Survey / site plan
□ 2 months bank statements (reserves)
□ Entity documents (if building in LLC)
□ Builder's risk insurance quote
□ Photo ID
□ Credit authorization form

If applicable:
□ Environmental report (Phase I — some lenders require)
□ Soil / geotechnical report
□ Flood zone determination
□ HOA approval (if building in an HOA community)
□ Pre-sale contracts or LOIs from future tenants/buyers (strengthens the deal)

Lender will order:
□ Appraisal (as-is lot value + as-complete value)
□ Title search + title insurance commitment
□ Flood certification

COMMERCIAL / MULTIFAMILY (5+ Units)

COMMERCIAL LOAN — DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Required:
□ Trailing 12-month operating statement (T-12) — income and expenses
□ Prior 2 years operating statements (if available)
□ Current rent roll — unit by unit, tenant names, lease terms, rent amounts
□ Executed purchase contract (or property details for refi)
□ Entity documents:
  □ Operating Agreement
  □ Articles of Organization
  □ EIN Letter
  □ Organizational chart (if multiple entities involved)
□ Personal Financial Statement (PFS) — for all guarantors/principals
□ Schedule of Real Estate Owned (SREO) — all properties owned
□ 2-3 months bank statements (personal and business)
□ Photo ID for all principals/guarantors
□ Property insurance quotes (commercial policy)
□ Management agreement (if third-party property management)
□ Credit authorization for all guarantors

If applicable:
□ Environmental Phase I report (typically required on commercial)
□ Existing survey
□ Existing appraisal (less than 6 months old)
□ Capital improvement history (recent renovations, deferred maintenance plan)
□ Utility bills (12 months — if owner pays utilities)
□ Property tax bills (current year)
□ Ground lease (if applicable)
□ Franchise/flag agreement (if hotel/hospitality)

Lender will order:
□ Commercial appraisal ($2,000-$5,000+)
□ Title search + title insurance commitment
□ Phase I environmental (if not provided — $2,000-$4,000)
□ Property condition assessment (PCA) — on larger deals

SBA 7(a) (Business Purpose — Real Estate Secured)

SBA 7(a) — DOCUMENT CHECKLIST

Business Documents:
□ Business plan (required for startups, recommended for existing)
□ 3 years business tax returns (complete, all schedules)
□ Year-to-date profit & loss statement
□ Year-to-date balance sheet
□ Business debt schedule (all outstanding loans/lines)
□ Articles of Organization / Incorporation
□ Operating Agreement / Bylaws
□ EIN Letter
□ Business licenses / permits
□ Ownership/affiliation chart

Personal Documents (for ALL owners with 20%+ ownership):
□ 3 years personal tax returns (complete, all schedules)
□ Personal Financial Statement (SBA Form 413)
□ Photo ID
□ Credit authorization
□ Resume

Real Estate Documents (if the loan includes real estate):
□ Purchase contract or current mortgage statement
□ Lease agreement (if tenant-occupied)
□ Property insurance
□ Environmental Phase I (may be required)

If franchise:
□ Franchise Disclosure Document (FDD)
□ Franchise agreement

If acquisition:
□ Purchase agreement / LOI
□ Seller's 3 years tax returns
□ Seller's financial statements
□ Equipment list with values

SBA-Specific Forms (lender provides these):
□ SBA Form 1919 (Borrower Information)
□ SBA Form 413 (Personal Financial Statement)
□ SBA Form 159 (Fee Disclosure — if packaging fee applies)
□ SBA Form 912 (Statement of Personal History)

DOCUMENT COLLECTION BEST PRACTICES

Make it EASY:

  1. Send the checklist as a numbered list — not a wall of text
  2. Let them text photos — many investors don't have scanners
  3. Accept bank statement screenshots — you can request official later
  4. Don't ask for everything at once if the deal is simple — DSCR only needs 4-5 items

Common problems and fixes:

| Problem | Fix | |---|---| | "I don't have a lease yet" | Use market rent estimate — AirDNA, Rentometer, or lender's own AVM | | "My LLC is new" | That's fine — most DSCR lenders accept new LLCs | | "I don't want to give my SSN" | "We don't pull credit until you authorize it. The form is just permission." | | "I'm self-employed" | "That's exactly why DSCR exists — we don't look at income at all." | | "I've been denied" | "Different lenders, different guidelines. Send me the denial letter and I'll figure out what went wrong." | | Bank statements have overdrafts | Address proactively — LOE letter explaining the overdrafts | | No track record (first flip) | Some lenders require 1+ projects, others don't. Match to the right lender. |

Follow-up if docs are slow:

| Day | Action | |---|---| | Day 2 | Text: "Hey [Name], just checking in — did you get a chance to gather those docs?" | | Day 4 | Call: "I want to keep your deal moving. Which items are you stuck on? I can help." | | Day 7 | Text: "Want me to hold your deal open? If timing changed, no worries — just let me know." | | Day 14 | Final: "Closing your file for now — reach out anytime when you're ready to move forward." |

Document storage:

  • Create a folder per deal: D:/DandyDon/deals/[borrower_name]_[date]/
  • Sub-folders: /docs, /lender_submissions, /conditions, /closing
  • NEVER store SSNs in plain text — credit auth forms go straight to the lender portal
