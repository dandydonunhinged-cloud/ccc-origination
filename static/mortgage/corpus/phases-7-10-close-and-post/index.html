PHASES 7-10: CLEAR TO CLOSE → CLOSING → POST-CLOSE → PIPELINE

The finish line, the paycheck, and the machine that keeps running

PHASE 7: CLEAR TO CLOSE

When CTC drops:

Immediate actions (within 2 hours):

  1. Review the settlement statement / closing disclosure
  • Verify loan amount, rate, term match what was quoted
  • Verify YOUR comp is correct:
  • Borrower-paid origination points: $[X]
  • Lender-paid YSP: $[X] (may show as "broker compensation" or "lender credit")
  • Processing fee: $[X]
  • Admin fee: $[X]
  • Verify borrower's cash to close is correct
  • Check for any surprise lender fees that weren't disclosed
  1. Coordinate with title company
  • Confirm closing date, time, location
  • Confirm title company has the closing package from the lender
  • Confirm title insurance premium and endorsements
  • Insurance referral: If you have a referral arrangement, confirm with the insurance agent that the policy is bound and your referral is documented
  • Get wire instructions FROM the title company (never create your own)
  1. Notify the borrower

    2. > "Hey [Name], great news — your loan is clear to close! Here's what happens next: > > Closing date: [date] > Location: [title company name + address] > Time: [time] > > What to bring: > - Government-issued photo ID > - Cashier's check for $[cash to close] payable to [title company name] > (OR wire instructions — I'll send those separately from the title company) > - Entity documents (if closing in LLC): [specific docs] > > Wire fraud warning: NEVER trust wire instructions received via email alone. > Always call the title company directly to verify before wiring funds. > Use a phone number you looked up yourself, not one from an email. > > Questions? Call me: [phone]"

  1. Wire instructions handling
  • Get wire instructions from the title company directly
  • Forward to borrower via email
  • CALL the borrower and confirm they received it
  • Tell them to CALL the title company to verify before wiring
  • Wire fraud is the #1 risk in real estate closings — protect your client and yourself

PHASE 8: CLOSING

Day of closing:

Pre-closing (morning of):

  • [ ] Confirm with title company: "Are we still on for [time] today?"
  • [ ] Confirm with borrower: "See you at [time] — have everything you need?"
  • [ ] If remote close (mobile notary): confirm notary is scheduled

At closing:

  • [ ] You do NOT need to attend every closing (most brokers don't)
  • [ ] If you attend: review docs with borrower, answer questions, be helpful
  • [ ] If you don't attend: be available by phone for any questions during signing
  • [ ] Signing takes 30-60 minutes typically
  • [ ] Borrower signs: promissory note, deed of trust/mortgage, closing disclosure (if provided), entity resolutions (if LLC), hazard insurance acknowledgment, title documents

Post-signing same day:

  • [ ] Title company sends signed package to lender for review
  • [ ] Lender reviews and authorizes funding (same day or next day)
  • [ ] Title company disburses funds:
  • Seller (purchase) or payoff (refinance)
  • Lender origination fees
  • Title/escrow fees
  • Insurance premiums
  • YOUR COMP (broker fees, processing, YSP)
  • Property taxes (if escrowed)

If funding is delayed:

  • Most business purpose loans fund same day or next day
  • If delayed: call the lender and the title company
  • Common delay reasons: lender QC review, missing satisfaction for payoff, title issues discovered at signing
  • Push hard — every day of delay costs the borrower (per diem interest)

PHASE 9: POST-CLOSE

The money:

Confirm your comp (Day 1-3 after funding):

  • [ ] Check your bank account
  • [ ] YSP: typically wired within 1-3 business days after funding
  • [ ] Borrower-paid points: disbursed at closing, should hit your account via title within 1-2 days
  • [ ] Processing/admin fees: same as above — from the closing disbursement

If comp is wrong or missing:

  • Call title company first — they cut the checks
  • If it's a lender-paid YSP issue: call your AE
  • Document everything — you have a signed broker fee agreement

Referral ask (don't be shy):

Every closed deal should generate 1-2 referrals. If you don't ask, you don't get.

Google review ask:

One Google review with keywords ("DSCR loan," "investment property," "great experience") is worth more than $500 in SEO. Ask every client.

The second closing:

Set calendar reminders for every applicable deal:

| Original Loan | Second Closing Type | When to Follow Up | Expected Comp | |---|---|---|---| | Bridge (purchase) | DSCR refi (after stabilization) | 3-6 months after close | $5,000-$10,000 | | Fix & flip (bridge) | DSCR refi (after rehab + tenant) | 6-12 months after close | $5,000-$10,000 | | Construction | Permanent financing (construction-to-perm) | At certificate of occupancy | $7,500-$15,000 | | Hard money | Any takeout (DSCR, conventional, bank) | 6-12 months | $5,000-$10,000 |

Follow-up template for second closing:

"Hey [Name], checking in on [property address]. How's the [rehab / stabilization / construction] going? When you're ready to refinance into permanent financing, I can run your numbers again — DSCR rates have [improved / held steady] since you closed. Let me know when you want to look at it."

This is free money. You already have the borrower. You already have their docs. The second closing takes 25% of the effort for the same comp.

Referral partner follow-up:

If the deal came from a referral partner:

  • [ ] Pay the referral fee within 10 business days of funding (per your agreement)
  • [ ] Text the partner: "Hey [Name], [borrower] closed on [date]. Your referral fee of $[X] is on its way. Thanks for the deal — keep them coming!"
  • [ ] This text does more for your pipeline than any ad you'll ever run

WEEKLY REVIEW (30 minutes, Friday afternoon)

Questions to answer:

  1. How many new leads came in this week? From where?
  2. How many did I contact within 15 minutes?
  3. How many files are in UW? Any stuck?
  4. How many are closing next week?
  5. What was my conversion rate this week? (leads → contacted → docs → submitted → closed)
  6. Which referral partners sent deals? Did I thank them?
  7. Which content performed on social? What should I post more of?
  8. Am I hitting my monthly deal target?
  9. Are there second-closing opportunities coming due?
  10. What's one thing I can do better next week?

Monthly:

  • Total revenue vs target
  • Lead source ROI — double down on what works, cut what doesn't
  • Referral partner health — who's active, who's gone cold, who needs attention
  • Content performance — which videos/posts generated leads
  • Lender performance — which lenders are fast, which are slow, which pay best
  • Comp optimization — am I leaving money on the table?
