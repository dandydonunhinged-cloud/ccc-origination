ClickClickClose.click — SEO Keyword Research (March 2026)

Executive Summary

This research covers 10 keyword clusters for investment property lending. DSCR loans are the breakout product of 2025-2026, with origination volumes up 123% YoY and non-QM securitization at record highs. The keyword landscape is competitive at the head terms but wide open for long-tail, intent-rich, and local variations. The biggest opportunity for clickclickclose.click is targeting the mid-funnel investor who already knows what product they need but hasn't chosen a lender yet.

Content Gap Analysis — Opportunities Nobody Is Doing Well

1. Bridge-to-DSCR Refinance Guide

Gap: Investors constantly search for how to refinance out of hard money/bridge into DSCR. Only Gelt Financial has a decent page. A comprehensive guide with timeline, checklist, and rate comparison would dominate. Target keyword: "bridge to DSCR refinance" / "refinance hard money to DSCR" Volume: 500-1,000 (growing fast) Competition: Very Low

2. DSCR Loan for Short-Term Rentals (Airbnb/VRBO)

Gap: Most DSCR content focuses on long-term rentals. STR investors need different info — projected rental income acceptance, platform documentation requirements, seasonal markets. Target keyword: "DSCR loan for Airbnb" / "short term rental loan" Volume: 1,500-3,000 Competition: Low-Medium

3. Investment Property Loan Comparison Calculator

Gap: No one has a good interactive tool that compares DSCR vs. conventional vs. hard money vs. bridge side by side with actual rate inputs. Target keyword: "investment property loan comparison" / "DSCR loan calculator" Volume: 3,000-5,000 combined Competition: Medium (but no good tools exist)

4. State-by-State Investment Property Lending Guides

Gap: Most lender sites have generic national pages. State-specific content targeting "DSCR loan [state]" or "hard money lender [state]" is thin. Target: 50 state pages + top 25 metro pages Volume: 300-2,000 per page Competition: Low per page, massive aggregate traffic

5. BRRRR Method Financing Guide

Gap: BiggerPockets owns the BRRRR concept but doesn't have lending-specific content. A "how to finance each step of BRRRR" guide connecting bridge, rehab, DSCR refinance would rank. Target keyword: "BRRRR financing" / "BRRRR method loan" Volume: 1,500-3,000 Competition: Low

6. LLC Property Financing

Gap: "Can I get a mortgage in an LLC?" is asked constantly. Most answers are vague. A definitive guide to entity-based investment property lending is missing. Target keyword: "investment property loan for LLC" / "DSCR loan for LLC" Volume: 1,500-3,000 combined Competition: Low

7. First-Time Real Estate Investor Financing Guide

Gap: Most content assumes experience. A genuine beginner's guide to investment property financing with decision trees would capture huge search volume. Target keyword: "first time real estate investor loan" / "how to finance first investment property" Volume: 2,000-4,000 Competition: Medium (but current content is weak)

8. Office-to-Residential Conversion Financing

Gap: Massive 2026 trend with $936B in CRE maturities. Almost no content exists specifically about financing these conversions. Target keyword: "office to residential conversion loan" / "adaptive reuse financing" Volume: 300-600 (but growing exponentially) Competition: Very Low
