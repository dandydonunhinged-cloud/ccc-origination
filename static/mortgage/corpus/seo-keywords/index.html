Mortgage SEO Keywords — Dallas/DFW Market

"YES Where Everyone Else Said NO" Positioning

Last Updated: 2026-03-10

Strategy Overview

Target audience: Borrowers who have been DENIED a mortgage or are struggling to qualify. Market: Dallas, Texas / DFW Metroplex Positioning: The loan officer who finds a way when others won't.

Key stat: Over 20% of mortgage applications were rejected in 2024 (Federal Reserve Bank of NY). The #1 reason for denial is high DTI (40% of denials), followed by low credit score (23%), unverifiable income (12%), and insufficient reserves (12%). This is the addressable market.

SEO principle: Long-tail keywords make up 91.8% of all search queries. They have lower volume but MUCH higher conversion rates. A denied borrower searching "FHA manual underwrite Dallas" is ready to act — they just need someone who will say yes.

Technical SEO Notes

Google Business Profile (Critical for Local)

  • Claim and optimize GBP listing for each office location
  • Primary category: "Mortgage Lender"
  • Secondary categories: "Mortgage Broker", "Loan Agency"
  • Post weekly updates (loan closings, market tips, program announcements)
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours
  • Add Q&A section with common denied-borrower questions

Schema Markup

  • LocalBusiness schema on all pages
  • FAQPage schema on blog posts
  • LoanOrCredit schema on service pages
  • Review schema (aggregate rating)

Content Requirements for EEAT (Experience, Expertise, Authority, Trust)

  • Author bio on every blog post with NMLS number
  • Link to NMLS Consumer Access page
  • Include real case studies (anonymized) of denied-then-approved borrowers
  • Get cited/linked by local news, real estate sites, financial blogs
  • Publish on the company domain, not third-party platforms

Page Speed

  • Target Core Web Vitals scores: LCP < 2.5s, FID < 100ms, CLS < 0.1
  • Mobile-first design (60%+ of mortgage searches are mobile)

Competitive Advantage Summary

The "YES where everyone else said NO" positioning works because:

  1. Most loan officers avoid difficult files. They want clean 740+ credit, W-2 income, 20% down. The difficult files get sent away. Those borrowers then search Google for help.
  1. Manual underwriting is the moat. Very few lenders do manual underwrites. Being the one who does — and ranking for those keywords — captures borrowers that other LOs literally cannot serve.
  1. Non-QM products expand the net. Bank statement loans, DSCR loans, and asset-based lending serve borrowers that don't fit the conventional box at all.
  1. Dallas DPA programs are under-marketed. Up to $60K in assistance that most borrowers don't know exists. Content about these programs captures first-time buyers early in their journey.
  1. Long-tail keywords have almost zero competition. "FHA manual underwrite Dallas" has maybe 2-3 pages competing for it. One solid page can rank #1 within weeks.

Recommended SEO Tools for Ongoing Keyword Research

  • Google Keyword Planner (free with Google Ads account) — actual search volume data
  • Google Search Console — see what queries your site already appears for
  • Google Trends — compare keyword popularity over time, regional interest
  • Ubersuggest (free tier available) — keyword ideas + volume estimates
  • AnswerThePublic — question-based keyword ideas from real searches
  • SEMrush or Ahrefs (paid) — competitor analysis, keyword gap analysis, volume + difficulty scores
  • AlsoAsked.com — maps "People Also Ask" questions by topic
