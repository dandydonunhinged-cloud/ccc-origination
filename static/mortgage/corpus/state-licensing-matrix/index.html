State Licensing Matrix: Business Purpose Loans Secured by 1-4 Unit Residential Property

Last Updated: March 11, 2026

The Key Legal Distinction

The federal SAFE Act defines "residential mortgage loan" as a loan primarily for personal, family, or household use secured by a dwelling. Business purpose loans (DSCR, fix-and-flip, bridge, hard money) secured by residential property are NOT consumer loans under the federal definition. However, state law controls licensing, and states vary enormously. Approximately 30 states + DC exempt business purpose loans. Approximately 10 states have some restriction. 10 states require licensing.

RED STATES (10): License Required

| State | License Type | Key Details | |-------|-------------|-------------| | Arizona | Mortgage Banker/Broker License | Qualified individual in AZ, 3+ yrs experience, $100K net worth, brick-and-mortar office, MLOs required. One of most restrictive. | | California | CFL or DRE Real Estate Broker | Key exemption: Unlicensed lender can fund loans arranged by licensed DRE broker. CFL min $25K net worth. De minimis: 1 commercial loan/12 months. | | Idaho | Mortgage Broker/Lender License | Required when collateral is 1-4 residential regardless of purpose. Commercial/5+ unit exempt. | | Minnesota | Limited Real Estate Broker License | Required for brokering BP loans on 1-4 residential. | | Nevada | Mortgage Company License | Brick-and-mortar in NV, qualified employee with 2+ yrs experience residing in NV, MLOs required. Extremely restrictive. | | North Dakota | Money Broker License | Broad "money brokering" definition covers consumer and commercial. ND Century Code 13-04.1. | | Oregon | Mortgage Broker/Lender License | Does NOT care about loan purpose -- only collateral type. If 1-4 residential = license required regardless of business purpose. | | South Dakota | Mortgage Lender License | License required regardless of collateral. De minimis: up to 5 loans on multifamily/commercial, aggregate under $4M/year. | | Utah | Mortgage Broker/Lender License | Required for BP loans on 1-4 residential. Commercial/5+ exempt. | | Vermont | Lender License | Required if loan under $1,000,000. Loans $1M+ may be exempt. |

YELLOW STATES (10): Conditional/Registration Required

| State | Condition | Key Exemption | |-------|----------|---------------| | Alabama | CONDITIONAL | May trigger licensing depending on structure. Verify with AL Banking Dept. | | Alaska | CONDITIONAL | Required if borrower is individual. Exempt if borrower is LLC/Corp. | | Florida | CONDITIONAL | Exempt if: entity borrower, loans sold to institutional investors, or individual using own funds for own investment. Misrepresenting consumer as BP is a FELONY. | | Georgia | CONDITIONAL | Required if borrower is natural person. Exempt if borrower is LLC/Corp. | | Michigan | CONDITIONAL | May require license under Mortgage Brokers Act for 1-4 residential. Complex -- verify with MI DIFS. | | New York | RE BROKER LICENSE | No mortgage license needed, but Real Estate Broker license required to broker BP loans. De minimis: 3 loans/yr. BPL Broker Attestation required. | | New Jersey | ATTESTATION | No standard mortgage license. Business Purpose Broker Attestation required. New nonbank servicer law. | | North Carolina | REGISTRATION | No license, but Loan Broker Registration with Secretary of State required (statute 66-106 to 66-112). Disclosure + surety bond/trust account. | | Virginia | CONDITIONAL | Likely exempt if borrower is LLC/Corp. Mortgage licensing applies to personal/family/household purpose loans. Verify with VA Bureau of Financial Institutions. | | Washington | CONDITIONAL | CLA business purpose exemption exists but does NOT apply if secured by borrower's primary residence. Non-owner-occupied investment property should be exempt. Licensed broker may satisfy requirements. |

GREEN STATES (30 + DC): No License Required

AR, CO, CT, DE, DC, HI, IL, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, ME, MD, MA, MS, MO, MT, NE, NH, NM, OH, OK, PA, RI, SC, TN, TX, WV, WI, WY

Notable details:

  • Colorado: Explicit statutory exemption -- "residential mortgage loan" = personal/family/household use only
  • Texas: Massive investor market, no license for non-owner-occupied BP loans
  • Illinois: Exempt but may need exemption certificate in Cook/Kane/Will/Peoria counties
  • Nebraska: Explicitly states BP loan is NOT a residential mortgage loan even if secured by dwelling
  • Hawaii: Licensing eliminated for BP residential mortgages since 1/1/2011
  • Rhode Island: License only if loan to natural person AND under $25K
  • Louisiana: Business/commercial/agricultural credit explicitly excluded from Consumer Credit Law

Critical Nuances

  1. Borrower entity type matters: In AK, FL, GA, VA -- use LLC borrowers to avoid licensing
  2. Licensed broker exemptions: CA, OR, WA allow unlicensed lenders if a licensed broker originates
  3. Collateral-based vs. purpose-based: RED states define by collateral type; GREEN states by loan purpose
  4. De minimis exemptions: VT ($1M+), SD (5 loans/$4M), CA (1/yr), NY (3/yr), RI ($25K+)
  5. Foreign entity registration: SOS registration may be required separately from lending license
  6. NMLS overlays: Some wholesale lenders require NMLS number even in GREEN states

Operational Summary

  • Immediately operable: 30 states + DC (GREEN)
  • Operable with LLC borrowers: AK, FL, GA, VA (+4 = 34 states + DC)
  • Operable with registration: NC, NJ (+2 = 36 states + DC)
  • Operable with restrictions: AL, MI, WA (case by case)
  • Avoid without licensing: AZ, CA, ID, MN, NV, ND, OR, SD, UT, VT

Effective reach: 34-37 states + DC without any license.
