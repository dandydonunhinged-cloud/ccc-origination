Turn Times by Loan Type

Conventional Loans

  • Industry average: 42-43 days to close
  • Fastest option: UWM (19 days avg) or Rocket Pro TPO Fast 15 (15 business days guaranteed, conventional SFR only)
  • Standard wholesale: 25-35 days with clean file

FHA Loans

  • Industry average: 45-55 days to close
  • Why slower: FHA case number assignment, CAIVRS check, FHA appraisal requirements (more stringent than conventional), FHA-specific conditions
  • Best options: Freedom Mortgage (#1 FHA lender), UWM (BOLT supports FHA), A&D Mortgage (24hr turnaround claim)
  • FHA Streamline Refis: Faster (no appraisal typically required) — 20-30 days possible

VA Loans

  • Industry average: 53-55 days to close (some sources say 70+)
  • Why slower: VA appraisal (VA assigns appraiser, can't shop), Certificate of Eligibility verification, MPR (Minimum Property Requirements) inspection, VA-specific conditions
  • Best options: Freedom Mortgage (#1 VA lender), UWM
  • VA IRRRL (Streamline): Faster — no appraisal, no income verification — 20-30 days possible

Non-QM Loans

  • Industry average: 30-45 days (standard non-QM)
  • DSCR loans: 30-34 days average (Griffin Funding reported as fast as 6 days)
  • Bank statement loans: 30-45 days
  • Foreign national: 45-60+ days (additional documentation, compliance requirements)
  • Why variable: Manual underwriting common, complex income calculations, non-standard documentation
  • Best options: A&D Mortgage (24hr UW turnaround + non-QM AUS), Angel Oak (most established), Deephaven (AI-assisted)

USDA Loans

  • Industry average: 45-60+ days
  • Why slowest: Requires USDA approval AFTER lender underwriting (two-step process). USDA office turn times vary by region.

What Causes Delays

Borrower-Side Delays

  1. Incomplete documentation — Missing pay stubs, bank statements, tax returns
  2. Large unexplained deposits — Triggers source-of-funds conditions
  3. Job changes during process — New employment verification required
  4. New credit inquiries/debt — Changes DTI, may require re-underwriting
  5. Gaps in employment — Letters of explanation required
  6. Self-employment complexity — More documentation, manual calculations

Property-Side Delays

  1. Low appraisal — Renegotiation, additional comps, or deal restructure
  2. Appraisal scheduling — VA appraisals especially slow (VA assigns appraiser)
  3. Title issues — Liens, encumbrances, boundary disputes, missing assignments
  4. HOA/Condo issues — Condo project review, HOA questionnaire delays
  5. Property condition — Repairs required (especially FHA/VA with MPR/MPS requirements)
  6. Survey issues — Encroachments, easement conflicts

Lender-Side Delays

  1. Volume spikes — Rate drops trigger refi waves, overwhelming underwriting capacity
  2. Staffing — Underwriter shortages during peak periods
  3. Stacking conditions — Each round of conditions adds 2-5 days
  4. Investor overlays — Additional requirements beyond agency guidelines
  5. Compliance reviews — TRID timing, QM/ATR verification
  6. System issues — Technology outages, platform migrations

Third-Party Delays

  1. Title company — Slow title searches, complex chains of title
  2. Insurance — Flood zone determinations, HOI shopping delays
  3. Employer verification — Companies slow to respond to VOE/VOI
  4. Tax transcript delays — IRS 4506-C processing times
  5. HOA management — Slow to provide questionnaires, budgets, insurance certificates

How to Structure a File to Minimize Conditions

Pre-Submission Checklist (The "Clean File" Protocol)

  1. Run AUS before submission — Get DU/LP findings first, address every finding before sending to lender
  2. Complete 1003 with zero blanks — Every field filled, every explanation provided upfront
  3. 60 days bank statements — All pages, all accounts, all institutions (even if there's a blank page)
  4. 30 days pay stubs — Most recent, covering at least one full month
  5. 2 years W-2s + tax returns — Complete with all schedules and K-1s if applicable
  6. Document large deposits — Paper trail ready for anything over 50% of monthly income
  7. Letter of explanation preemptive — Employment gaps, credit events, name variations, address discrepancies
  8. Property insurance quote — Have HOI ready before submission
  9. Gift letters — If using gift funds, have donor's bank statement + gift letter + transfer proof ready
  10. Condo docs — If applicable, get HOA questionnaire and master insurance BEFORE submission

Income-Specific Tips

  • Self-employed: Include P&L (YTD), business license, CPA letter if available
  • Commission/bonus: Provide written VOE showing 2-year history of variable income
  • Rental income: Lease agreements + Schedule E + proof of receipt
  • DSCR: Current lease, rent roll, property cash flow analysis ready

Technology Leverage

  • Use lender's income calculator (UWM's AI tool, A&D's Non-QM AUS) to pre-validate income before submission
  • Use real-time compliance check tools (Pennymac POWER+) to catch issues early
  • Submit through lender portal (not email) for fastest processing
  • Upload documents in correct order and properly labeled

The Golden Rule

"A file that requires zero conditions on first pass closes 10-15 days faster than one with 5+ conditions."

Each round of conditions = 2-5 additional days. Three rounds of conditions can add 6-15 days. The fastest brokers are the ones who submit clean files, not the ones who submit first.

Strategic Recommendations

For Purchase Transactions (Speed Critical)

  1. UWM — 19-day average, BOLT for instant initial approval. The default choice for speed.
  2. Rocket Pro TPO — Fast 15 Guarantee if conventional SFR in eligible state. The insurance policy.
  3. A&D Mortgage — If non-QM borrower needs purchase speed, their 24-hr UW is the play.

For Refinances (Speed Helpful but Less Critical)

  1. UWM — Still fastest overall
  2. Freedom Mortgage — For FHA Streamline / VA IRRRL, their government expertise matters
  3. Any lender with competitive rate — Refi speed matters less than purchase speed

For Credit-Challenged Borrowers

  1. Carrington — Purpose-built for this segment, ProcessIQ automation, 2-day published turn times
  2. A&D Mortgage — Non-QM AUS handles non-traditional profiles
  3. Angel Oak — Most established non-QM, deep manual underwriting expertise

For Foreign National Borrowers

  1. A&D Mortgage — Dedicated foreign national programs, 24-hr turnaround claim, non-QM AUS
  2. Angel Oak — Established foreign national programs
  3. FNBA — Portfolio lender, can be flexible on unique scenarios

For DSCR/Investor Loans

  1. A&D Mortgage — Non-QM AUS + 24-hr turnaround
  2. Deephaven — AI document processing, 30-45 day timeline
  3. Angel Oak — Largest non-QM originator, deep investor loan experience
  4. The Loan Store — Major non-QM volume ($8.2B), fast-growing

For Government Loans (FHA/VA)

  1. Freedom Mortgage — #1 VA and FHA lender nationally. Deep expertise.
  2. UWM — BOLT supports FHA, fast overall
  3. A&D Mortgage — Post-Mr. Cooper acquisition, expanded government capability

Key Takeaways

  1. UWM is the speed king. 19 days average start-to-finish vs. 43-day industry average. BOLT gives 15-minute initial approvals. 17-day CTC average. No other lender is close on published metrics.
  1. Rocket Pro TPO has the only guaranteed turn time with teeth. Fast 15 = $2,500 credit if they miss 15 business days. But limited to conventional SFR in ~34 states.
  1. A&D Mortgage is the non-QM speed dark horse. 24-hour underwriting across ALL products including non-QM + the first non-QM AUS. Mr. Cooper acquisition makes them a full-spectrum player.
  1. Government loans (FHA/VA) inherently take longer. 53-70+ days average. This is structural (VA appraisal process, FHA requirements) — no lender can fully overcome it, but specialists like Freedom Mortgage navigate it best.
  1. Non-QM takes 30-45 days on average. Manual underwriting, complex income, non-standard docs all add time. A&D's Non-QM AUS is the biggest innovation here.
  1. Clean file submission is the #1 speed lever brokers control. A file with zero conditions closes 10-15 days faster than one with multiple rounds of conditions. Use lender income calculators, pre-run AUS, and document everything upfront.
  1. Most lenders don't publish turn times publicly. Many require broker login to see current numbers. This is by design — it allows them to adjust expectations based on current volume without public accountability.
  1. AI is the next frontier. UWM (BOLT/Income Calculator), Rocket (Pathfinder), A&D (Non-QM AUS), NewRez (HomeVision), Deephaven (Ocrolus) — every major player is investing in AI for speed. By late 2026, expect significantly faster turn times from lenders who execute well on AI.
  1. loanDepot is the wild card. Just relaunched wholesale March 9, 2026. No track record to evaluate. Monitor closely.
  1. Volume matters both ways. High market volume (projected $2.32T in 2026) will pressure turn times. Lenders with the best technology and staffing will maintain speed; others will slip.
