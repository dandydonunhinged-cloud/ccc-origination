Compiled: March 17, 2026 Purpose: Mortgage broker lender matrix for investment & commercial lending

CATEGORY LEGEND

  • DSCR = Debt Service Coverage Ratio (rental property, no income docs)
  • F&F = Fix and Flip / Bridge (short-term rehab)
  • CONST = Ground-Up Construction
  • CRE = Commercial Real Estate (multifamily 5+, mixed-use, office, retail)
  • SBA = SBA 7(a) / 504
  • NQM = Non-QM Residential (bank statement, asset depletion, foreign national)
  • HM = Hard Money / Private Lending
