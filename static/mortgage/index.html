Common questions

Before you submit

What documents do I need to start?

For most business-purpose programs: the property address, your target loan amount, the projected rent (or ARV for flips), and your entity name. Full document list comes after the first call. Most programs don't require tax returns, W-2s, or pay stubs.

How long does the whole process take?

DSCR purchase: 21–35 days close. Bridge: 7–14 days. Fix & flip: 10–21 days. Construction: 45–75 days. SBA: 45–90 days. We won't promise faster than the program allows — the lender sets the clock, we just keep the conditions clearing.

What credit score do I need?

Most DSCR programs want 680+. Some will go to 640 with more down. Bridge and fix & flip programs often start at 660. Below 640, the deal needs compensating factors (more equity, lower LTV, larger reserves). The lender product matrix has the full per-program breakdown.

Do you only work with investors?

Yes. This is a 100% investor-focused shop. We don't do primary residence, FHA, or VA. If you're buying a home to live in, we can refer you to someone who specializes in that.

What does it cost?

Borrower-paid origination and lender-paid YSP. The 5-pass analysis is free, the call is free, the lender shopping is free. The only cost to you is what's in the closing disclosure — disclosed up front, no surprises. See the broker compensation doc for the full breakdown.
