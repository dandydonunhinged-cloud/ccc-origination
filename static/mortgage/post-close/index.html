Post-close — the 90-day window, the next deal

The 90-day window. Servicing transfer. The 30/90/180-day rate-reset check. The relationship is the asset. The next deal is the metric.

What this stage is: Servicing transfer, 90-day rate-reset, next-deal.

PHASE 9: POST-CLOSE

The money:

Confirm your comp (Day 1-3 after funding):

  • [ ] Check your bank account
  • [ ] YSP: typically wired within 1-3 business days after funding
  • [ ] Borrower-paid points: disbursed at closing, should hit your account via title within 1-2 days
  • [ ] Processing/admin fees: same as above — from the closing disbursement

If comp is wrong or missing:

  • Call title company first — they cut the checks
  • If it's a lender-paid YSP issue: call your AE
  • Document everything — you have a signed broker fee agreement

Record the deal:

Referral ask (don't be shy):

Every closed deal should generate 1-2 referrals. If you don't ask, you don't get.

Google review ask:

One Google review with keywords ("DSCR loan," "investment property," "great experience") is worth more than $500 in SEO. Ask every client.

The second closing:

Set calendar reminders for every applicable deal:

| Original Loan | Second Closing Type | When to Follow Up | Expected Comp | |---|---|---|---| | Bridge (purchase) | DSCR refi (after stabilization) | 3-6 months after close | $5,000-$10,000 | | Fix & flip (bridge) | DSCR refi (after rehab + tenant) | 6-12 months after close | $5,000-$10,000 | | Construction | Permanent financing (construction-to-perm) | At certificate of occupancy | $7,500-$15,000 | | Hard money | Any takeout (DSCR, conventional, bank) | 6-12 months | $5,000-$10,000 |

Follow-up template for second closing:

"Hey [Name], checking in on [property address]. How's the [rehab / stabilization / construction] going? When you're ready to refinance into permanent financing, I can run your numbers again — DSCR rates have [improved / held steady] since you closed. Let me know when you want to look at it."

This is free money. You already have the borrower. You already have their docs. The second closing takes 25% of the effort for the same comp.

Referral partner follow-up:

If the deal came from a referral partner:
